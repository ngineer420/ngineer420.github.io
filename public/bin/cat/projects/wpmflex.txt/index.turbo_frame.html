Project: wpmflex.com

A typing speed test: measure your words per minute and accuracy over 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds, track your best scores and see how you compare.

Live at wpmflex.com

Stack

  • - HTML/CSS/JS: Plain, no framework or build step
  • - DevOps: Static files on GitHub Pages, custom domain (wpmflex.com)

Objective

To offer a fast, no-signup way to measure typing speed and accuracy across a few durations and keep a personal best.

Benefits

  • - Runs entirely in the browser; no download and no sign-up
  • - Choose a 15, 30, 60 or 120 second test
  • - Tracks WPM, accuracy and your best scores
  • - No account, no limits

Drawbacks

  • - Best scores are stored in your browser, so they don't follow you across devices
