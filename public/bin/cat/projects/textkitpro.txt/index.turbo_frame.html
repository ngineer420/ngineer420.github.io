Project: textkitpro.com

Four text tools in one page: a live word counter with keyword density, a case converter, a Lorem Ipsum generator and a line-by-line diff checker.

Live at textkitpro.com

Stack

  • - HTML/CSS/JS: Plain, no framework or build step
  • - DevOps: Static files on GitHub Pages, custom domain (textkitpro.com)

Objective

To gather the everyday text chores writers and developers repeat — counting, re-casing, filler text and diffing — into one fast page that runs offline in the tab.

Benefits

  • - Everything runs in the browser; your text never leaves the page
  • - Word count with keyword density updates live as you type
  • - Case converter covers camelCase, snake_case and more
  • - No account, no limits

Drawbacks

  • - The diff checker compares line by line, not word by word

Easter Eggs

  • - eRabb.it, tucked into the very bottom-right corner of the page
