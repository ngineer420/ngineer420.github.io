Project: flicktrainer.com

A browser-based FPS aim trainer: click targets as fast and as accurately as you can, then track your reaction time, accuracy and best score.

Live at flicktrainer.com

Stack

  • - HTML/CSS/JS: Plain, no framework or build step
  • - DevOps: Static files on GitHub Pages, custom domain (flicktrainer.com)

Objective

To give players a quick, no-download way to warm up their mouse aim and measure click speed and accuracy before a match.

Benefits

  • - Runs entirely in the browser; no download and no sign-up
  • - Tracks reaction time, accuracy and your best score
  • - Instant restart to keep grinding runs
  • - No account, no limits

Drawbacks

  • - A flat 2D target field, not a full 3D FPS scenario
