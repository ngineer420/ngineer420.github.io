Project: hardwarecheckup.com

Browser-based hardware tests: check your webcam, microphone, speakers, keyboard, mouse and screen for dead pixels — all on your own device.

Live at hardwarecheckup.com

Stack

  • - HTML/CSS/JS: Plain, no framework or build step
  • - DevOps: Static files on GitHub Pages, custom domain (hardwarecheckup.com)

Objective

To let anyone confirm a webcam, mic, speakers, keyboard, mouse or display actually work — quickly, without installing diagnostic software.

Benefits

  • - Every test runs on your device; nothing is uploaded
  • - Handy before a video call, or when checking used hardware
  • - No download and no sign-up
  • - Covers dead pixels, input devices and audio in one place

Drawbacks

  • - Camera and microphone tests need browser permission to run

Easter Eggs
