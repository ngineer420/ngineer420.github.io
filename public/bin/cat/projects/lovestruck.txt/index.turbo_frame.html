Project: lovestruck.us

Date ideas, gift guides and relationship advice

Live at lovestruck.us

Stack

  • - HTML/CSS/JS: Plain, no framework or build step
  • - DevOps: Static files on GitHub Pages, custom domain via Cloudflare DNS

Objective

To publish date night ideas, anniversary and Valentine's Day gift guides, and honest relationship advice, plus a love languages quiz that runs in the browser.

Benefits

  • - Long-form guides with no sign-up and no paywall
  • - The quiz keeps its answers in the browser and sends nothing to a server
  • - No backend or database, so the whole site is free to host

Drawbacks

  • - With no build step, a change to shared markup means a change to every page

Easter Eggs

  • - eRabb.it, tucked into the very bottom-right corner of the page
