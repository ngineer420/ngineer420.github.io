Project: Perfect Tune

A musician's toolkit — a microphone tuner, a metronome and a tone generator — running on the Web Audio API.

Live at perfecttune.net

Stack

  • - HTML/CSS/JS: Plain, no framework or build step
  • - DevOps: Static files on GitHub Pages, custom domain (perfecttune.net)

Objective

To give players a browser-based tuner (real-time pitch detection from the mic), a metronome, and a tone/drone generator, without installing an app.

Benefits

  • - Real-time pitch detection runs in the browser; mic audio never leaves the device
  • - Metronome and tone generator built on the Web Audio API
  • - No app, no account, works on phones and laptops
  • - The name says it: pitch (the tuner) plus beat (the metronome)

Drawbacks

  • - Tuner accuracy depends on your microphone and how quiet the room is
