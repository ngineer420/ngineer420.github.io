Projects

A family of free, fast, browser-based tools — every one runs entirely client-side with no sign-up. Built and maintained under the eRabb.it banner.

  • Blank Notepad — a distraction-free notepad that autosaves in your browser (blanknotepad.com)
  • Calculator Euphoria — free online calculators for math, finance, health & everyday life (calculatoreuphoria.com)
  • Chimp Memory — the working-memory game chimpanzees beat humans at, plus sequence, number & visual tests (chimpmemory.com)
  • Clock Lab — countdown, stopwatch, pomodoro, alarms & world clock (clocklab.net)
  • CPS Boost — click speed test: measure your clicks per second (cpsboost.com)
  • DevBox Kit — JSON formatter, Base64 & URL encoder, timestamp converter & regex tester (devboxkit.com)
  • Draw Lots — spinner wheel, name picker, dice, coin flip & more (drawlots.net)
  • eRabb.it — the terminal you're poking around in right now (erabb.it)
  • Flick Trainer — FPS aim trainer: click targets and track reaction time, accuracy & best score (flicktrainer.com)
  • Fontloom — fancy text generator: stylish Unicode from plain text (fontloom.com)
  • Gamut Lens — color picker, converters, gradients & contrast checks (gamutlens.com)
  • Hardware Checkup — test your webcam, mic, speakers, keyboard, mouse & screen for dead pixels (hardwarecheckup.com)
  • In ASCII — turn text and images into ASCII art (inascii.com)
  • Paper Printouts — printable graph paper, worksheets & templates, generated to your measurements (paperprintouts.com)
  • Perfect Tune — guitar tuner, metronome & tone generator (perfecttune.net)
  • Photo Shrink — resize, compress, crop & convert images in your browser (photoshrink.net)
  • QR Mint — generate QR codes for links, WiFi, contacts & text (qrmint.net)
  • Reflex Zap — reaction time test: how fast are your reflexes? (reflexzap.com)
  • Stick Drift Check — controller stick drift test with dead zones, buttons, triggers & rumble (stickdriftcheck.com)
  • TextKit Pro — word counter, case converter, Lorem Ipsum generator & diff checker (textkitpro.com)
  • WPM Flex — typing speed test: words per minute and accuracy over 15 to 120 seconds (wpmflex.com)
